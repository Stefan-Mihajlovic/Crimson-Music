# Crimson icon direction — Soundfold

Research and selection: November 2026. The brief was a distinctive music-oriented emblem in the app’s violet accent, with an editable Icon Composer source. The selected direction is a folded sound wave.

## Comparison

Current app artwork was inspected through Apple’s App Store listings, alongside official brand guidance where available. These are observations of the reviewed artwork, not assets to reuse.

| Service | Visual device | Lesson for Crimson |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Apple Music](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/apple-music/id1108187390) | Paired musical notes; strong monochrome silhouette | Musical meaning can be immediate, but another paired-note symbol would be difficult to distinguish. |
| [Spotify](https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/design) | Three curved signal strokes inside a circle | Repetition and a simple container work at small sizes; avoid a similar stack of arcs. |
| [Deezer](https://newsroom-deezer.com/2023/11/deezer-new-brand-identity-and-logo/) | A purple waveform heart | Combining audio and emotion creates an emblem; violet alone cannot distinguish Crimson. |
| [YouTube Music](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/youtube-music/id1017492454) | Play triangle, concentric ring, red disc | Excellent playback recognition, but the play-circle category is crowded. |
| [TIDAL](https://tidal.com/press) | Four diamonds arranged as a compact geometric mark | A small number of shapes can build recognition without literal music notation. |
| [SoundCloud](https://soundcloud.com/company/media-kit/) | Vertical audio strokes merging into a cloud | One coherent hybrid silhouette is stronger than multiple separate symbols. |
| [Amazon Music](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/amazon-music-songs-podcasts/id510855668) | Wordmark with the Amazon smile | Existing brand equity carries the mark; Crimson needs a symbol that works without text. |
| [Bandcamp](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bandcamp/id706408639) | Cyan slanted quadrilateral | Distinctive geometry and consistent use can carry an abstract identity. |
| [Qobuz](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/qobuz-music-editorial/id946429340) | White wordmark on a dark field | Typography can be recognizable, but tiny launcher icons benefit from a compact silhouette. |

## Selected composition

Soundfold turns a single rising and falling audio gesture into a folded ribbon. Its tall crest, diagonal front fold, and shorter returning echo give it an asymmetric rhythm. Open spaces define the overlaps even when the mark is monochrome. It does not depend on a letter, a play button, or conventional note stems.

The final vector was drawn specifically for Crimson after the folded-wave direction was selected from generated exploratory concepts. It is not a modified competitor logo. “Soundfold” names the design internally; the application remains **Crimson Music**.

## Material and platform treatment

Apple’s [app icon guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-icons) and [Icon Composer workflow](https://developer.apple.com/icon-composer/) informed the separation of foreground shapes from system-generated lighting and masks. Three SVG layers retain editable geometry. Apple supplies the glass treatment; flat exports use the same shapes for Android, web, and the splash screen.

The launch icon is deliberately text-free. Purple comes from Crimson’s existing accent palette. Small-size, monochrome, dark, and clear checks protect the silhouette; Android foreground padding protects it from launcher cropping.
